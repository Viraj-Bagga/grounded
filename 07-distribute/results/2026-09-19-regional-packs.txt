== licence, recorded per pack
  WHO text is CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO. The fact sheets print no licence line: the terms come from WHO's site-wide
  terms of use, checked 2026-09-19, and that is what the pack records. ShareAlike makes a pack containing WHO
  text CC BY-NC-SA as a whole; NonCommercial means it may not ship in a commercial product, which is a real
  constraint on the Seed Round pitch. Per-source licence status is still recorded per registry row, so the
  public-domain NHLBI rows inside africa-ssa remain identifiable. Each pack ships LICENCE.txt with the
  attribution lines WHO requires and the legal-code URL.
