=== what the run says the cause is
Two warnings from transformers, both in this run:

  `causal_conv1d_fn` is falling back to its reference PyTorch implementation
  because `causal_conv1d` is not installed.
  `mamba_chunk_scan_combined` is falling back to its reference PyTorch
  implementation because `mamba_ssm` is not installed.

That is the memory. 21 of the 42 blocks are Mamba2, and the reference chunked
scan materialises the per-chunk states the fused kernel never holds. With
chunk_size 256, 96 heads, head_dim 80 and 8 groups, one such intermediate is
gigabytes per layer at 1633 tokens, and gradient checkpointing does not help:
checkpointing frees activations BETWEEN layers, while the peak here is INSIDE
one layer's recompute.

The allocator flag worked and was not the limit: expandable_segments itself
logged mapping failures at 10 MB free, and the failing allocation was 168 MiB
against 104 MiB free. The card was genuinely full, not fragmented.

So attempt 1's diagnosis was half right. use_reentrant=False was a real bug and
is fixed: checkpointing now reports True. It was not the binding constraint.

FOR ATTEMPT 3, cheapest first:
  1. chunk_size at load. config.json sets 256; it is an implementation detail of
     the chunked scan, mathematically equivalent at any value, and the fallback's
     largest intermediate scales with it. from_pretrained(..., chunk_size=64).
     Free, no effect on what is learned. Probe 256, 128, 64, 32 in one run, each
     about a minute, and take the first that fits with headroom.
  2. Install mamba_ssm and causal_conv1d. This is the proper fix and it is also
     the fast path; the same warnings say the fallbacks are "much slower", which
     is a cost problem as well as a memory one. Risk: a CUDA source build against
     torch 2.14+cu130, 10 to 30 minutes on the meter, and it may not have wheels.
  3. An 80 GB card. Certain on memory, roughly 2 to 3 times the rate, and still
     slow without the kernels.
  NOT shortening the prompts: their length is the retrieved context, per Viraj.

Also measure step time in the next probe. If the fallback is slow enough, 2
epochs over 124 examples may cost more than the card upgrade would.
