1. THE EMERGENCY NUMBER. Seven chunks hardcode a US number.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CP-ACS-004   3 mentions   "Call 9-1-1 for emergency medical care, even if you
                             are not sure that you're having a heart attack."
  CP-ACS-005   1            "If you don't know whether your chest pain is angina
                             or a heart attack, call 9-1-1."
  CP-ACS-006   1            "It is important to call 9-1-1 if you have these
                             symptoms."
  CP-ANG-001   1            "If you can't tell whether your chest pain is a heart
                             attack, call 9-1-1 right away."
  CP-ANG-002   1            "If you have chest pain that does not go away, call
                             9-1-1 immediately."
  CP-ANG-003   1            "Call 9-1-1 if you feel chest discomfort that does not
                             go away with rest or medicine."
  CP-PERI-001  1            "If you have chest pain or severe shortness of breath,
                             or your symptoms get worse, call 9-1-1 or seek
                             medical help right away."

  ONE MORE, WHICH NEEDS NOTHING. CP-ANG-010 calls unstable angina "a medical
  emergency" and names no number. No other chunk mentions 911, 999, 112 or any
  other number.

  THE MODEL REPEATS IT, AND NARROWLY. Across 75 saved model results:
    next_steps   14 results, and EXACTLY ONE distinct string in all 14:
                   "Call 9-1-1 immediately"
    rationale    13 results, 8 distinct sentences, every one of the form
                   "The retrieved context ... says to call 9-1-1 if ..."

  THE TWO ARE NOT THE SAME CASE.
    In next_steps it INSTRUCTS THE PATIENT and is wrong outside the US.
    In the rationale it REPORTS WHAT A US SOURCE SAYS, which is true. The
    chunk is shown unedited and the citation carries the regional annotation
    already. Substituting inside that sentence would misquote a source this
    project deliberately does not edit. Viraj's call 2026-09-20: leave it.

  NOTHING TO REPLACE IN THE DISPOSITION. DISPOSITION.red is already "Call
  emergency services now" and names no number, and neither does
  RAISED_RED_STEPS. So the work there is an ADDITION, not a substitution.

2. WHILE-YOU-WAIT GUIDANCE. One chunk in thirty-five.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CP-ACS-004 IS THE ONLY CHUNK IN THE CORPUS that says anything about the
  minutes between calling for help and help arriving:

    "An ambulance is the best and safest way to get to the hospital."
    "Do not drive to the hospital or let someone else drive you."
    "Every minute matters."
    "EMS personnel can check how you are doing and start tests and lifesaving
     medicines right away."
    "People who arrive by ambulance often get faster treatment at the hospital."

  EIGHT OF NINE CONDITIONS HAVE NONE.
    CP-ACS    6 chunks   CP-ACS-004 only
    CP-ANG   12 chunks   none
    CP-DIFF   1          none
    CP-GERD   3          none
    CP-PANIC  3          none
    CP-PE     4          none
    CP-PERI   2          none
    CP-PLEU   1          none
    CP-PNA    3          none
  Everything those eight contain is "call someone", which is the disposition,
  not while-you-wait.

  NO POSITIONING ADVICE EXISTS ANYWHERE. Searched lean, sit up, upright, lie
  down, position, posture, loosen, elevate across all 35 chunks. ONE hit:

    CP-PERI-002: "Chest pain that feels sharp, gets worse with breathing, and
                  feels better with sitting up and leaning forward"

  That is a SYMPTOM DESCRIPTION, not advice. Turning it into "sit up and lean
  forward" is an inference the corpus does not make, and it is the same move
  constraint 14 forbids for contrast axes: not paraphrased, not implied, the
  actual sentence. Viraj's call 2026-09-20: leave it out.

  "REST" IS A DIAGNOSTIC CRITERION IN ALL 16 OF ITS MENTIONS, never advice.
  CP-ACS-005, CP-ANG-001/002/003/005/006/007/008/009/010/011/012, CP-PE-003.
  Every one is of the form "pain relieved by rest" or "pain during rest",
  which is what separates angina from a heart attack. Turning it into a step
  would be the constraint 11 failure exactly: chunk content placed in a field
  about what the patient should do.

  A MEDICATION TRAP IN THE ONE GOOD CHUNK. CP-ACS-004 also says "Never delay
  calling 9-1-1, taking aspirin or doing anything else you think might help."
  It names a drug. Not usable as a step, and the medication guard would drop
  it anyway. Not touched.

  WHAT EXISTS FOR THE "WHEN IT CHANGES" HALF, grounded:
    CP-DIFF-001  "Get immediate medical care if you have chest pain that does
                  not go away, crushing pain or pressure in the chest, or chest
                  pain along with nausea, sweating, dizziness"
    CP-PERI-001  "...or your symptoms get worse, call 9-1-1 or seek medical
                  help right away"
    CP-PE-001    "Call your healthcare provider right away if you think you may
                  have symptoms of VTE."
    CP-GERD-001  "...if your symptoms don't get better with over-the-counter
                  medicines or lifestyle changes"  (names medicines)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

                                   claim. Takes the region's number now.
  "Do not drive yourself."         GROUNDED. CP-ACS-004: "Do not drive to the
                                   hospital or let someone else drive you."

                                   not waiting. Invented, and it reads as
                                   sensible advice, which is what made it
                                   dangerous: every other clinical claim on the
                                   page carries a key and this one did not.
                                   CUT 2026-09-20, Viraj's call, his line.

  and R5), where that line is not grounded in what was cited. It is sound
  general advice for anyone being told to call an ambulance, but it is not
  cited for those cases. Worth a decision later.
