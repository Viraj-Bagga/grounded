--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT THIS DECIDES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  DECIDES: whether base.en-q5_1 still holds on a real voice in a real
  room. On the synthetic set it tied small.en at 3.2x less size, 2x less
  RAM and 2.2x the speed. If small pulls ahead HERE on extra1, which is
  the clinical vocabulary line, that is the case for spending the 181 MB.

  DOES NOT DECIDE: anything about a phone microphone, any other voice,
  or any other room. Five clips from one speaker is a sanity check on a
  choice already made, not a benchmark.

  A MISS IN extra2 IS THE WORST OUTCOME ON THIS PAGE. A dropped 'not' or
  a swapped left and right is a wrong verdict or a guard that does not
  fire, and no downstream check can catch it. The composer being
  editable is what stands between that and the screen.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS DRY RUN IS WORTH KEEPING ANYWAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  It is still synthetic, so it says nothing about a real room. But it is the
  first time the CLINICAL VOCABULARY LINE has been scored, and on it both base
  variants lose `nitroglycerin` (split as "nitro glycerin") and `ramipril`
  (as "ramepril"), while small.en and small.en-q5_1 get both exactly right.

  The 2026-09-19 model choice had base and small TIED at 2.45% and 2.10% WER.
  That set contained one drug name in one clip out of thirteen. This set
  contains a line built for it, and the tie does not survive contact with it.

  So the question the real recording has to answer is sharper than it was:
  not "is base still good enough" but "is small worth 181 MB against 57 MB,
  2x the RAM and 2.2x the time, to get drug names right". That is Viraj's
  call and it is a clinical one, because the app is a consumer triage tool
  that does not prescribe, and a patient reading "ramepril" in the composer
  can fix it before sending, which is the design.
