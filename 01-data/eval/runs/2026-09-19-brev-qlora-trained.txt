=== WHY NOT QLoRA, measured in four failed runs on this instance
With the fused Mamba2 kernel live, transformers' nemotron_h calls
mamba2_split_conv1d_scan_combined and hands it in_proj's and out_proj's raw
weight tensors, which the kernel multiplies itself. Under bitsandbytes those
weights are packed 4-bit blobs, so the matmul fails on shape:

  4-bit, batch 2:              mat1 and mat2 shapes cannot be multiplied (2752x7680 and 1x12042240)
  4-bit, batch 1:              same failure at (1283x7680 and 1x12042240)   -> not batch size
  4-bit, skip out_proj:        moves to in_proj (256x3136 and 1x205520896)
  4-bit, skip in_proj+out_proj: same in_proj failure; footprint rose 3.36 -> 5.09 GB, so the skip
                                partly applied, but the module the kernel touches stayed packed

So the choice on one 48 GB card was: fused kernels with a bf16 base, or a 4-bit
base with the reference fallbacks, which needed more than 47 GiB (attempts 1 and
2). bf16 plus kernels fits in 7.95 GB of weights and trains in 189 s.

WHAT IS UNCHANGED: the adapter, its six target modules, the 50 matched modules,
rank 16, alpha 32, 2 epochs, the data, and the merge to bf16. The Q in QLoRA was
a memory technique, and the memory came from the kernels instead. Nothing about
what was learned depends on it.

WHAT IS DIFFERENT: training saw unquantised weights, while the shipped model is
Q4_K_M. That gap existed before in the other direction and is what step 6
measures.
