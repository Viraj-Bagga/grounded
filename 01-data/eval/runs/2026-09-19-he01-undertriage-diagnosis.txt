# HE01: why the tuned model reads a heart attack down to yellow under retrieval
#
# date    2026-09-19, after the swap
# what    diagnosis only, nothing changed
# source  01-data/eval/runs/2026-09-19-heldout-{base,tuned}-{matched,retrieval}.jsonl
#         01-data/eval/heldout-eval/HELDOUT-EVAL-v1.json, 01-data/chunks/
# short   The chunk that reads it down is CP-ANG-003. The reason it can is that
#         CP-ACS-006 is missing. Retrieval is the fault, not the tune.

================================================================================
THE CASE
================================================================================
HE01, expected RED, condition CP-ACS. 63-year-old woman, high blood pressure,
amlodipine.

  "For about forty minutes my upper back and left shoulder have been aching, I
   get out of breath just sitting here, and I suddenly feel completely drained
   and a bit queasy. THERE IS NO REAL CHEST PAIN."

That last sentence is the whole case. It is the textbook atypical presentation
that kills women, and the set keys it to CP-ACS-006 for exactly that reason.

================================================================================
THE SPLIT, and it is deterministic
================================================================================
  condition          chunks in the prompt                         verdict
  tuned,  matched    CP-ACS-006, CP-ACS-003                       red 3/3
  tuned,  retrieval  CP-ACS-003, CP-PERI-002, CP-ANG-003          YELLOW 3/3
  base,   matched    CP-ACS-006, CP-ACS-003                       red 3/3
  base,   retrieval  CP-ACS-003, CP-PERI-002, CP-ANG-003          red 3/3

Retrieval DROPS CP-ACS-006 and ADDS CP-PERI-002 and CP-ANG-003.

================================================================================
1. WHICH CHUNK READS IT DOWN: CP-ANG-003
================================================================================
All three tuned retrieval runs cite CP-ACS-003 and CP-ANG-003 and reason about
ANGINA. CP-PERI-002 is retrieved and NEVER cited in any run on either model, so
pericarditis is not involved.

CP-ANG-003 matches this case symptom for symptom:

  "The pain or discomfort can also be felt in areas far from the source of the
   pain, such as the shoulders, arms, neck, back, and jaw"   -> back, shoulder
  "Shortness of breath"                                      -> breathless at rest
  "Extreme tiredness"                                        -> completely drained
  "Nausea, or feeling sick to the stomach"                   -> queasy
  "Weakness"                                                 -> drained

Every finding in the case appears in an angina symptom list. Nothing in the
retrieved set says those same findings are a heart attack WITHOUT chest pain.

================================================================================
2. THE MECHANISM: one trigger in the whole prompt, and it needs chest pain
================================================================================
Grepped all three retrieved chunks for a call to action:

  CP-ACS-003    no call to action at all
  CP-PERI-002   no call to action at all
  CP-ANG-003    "Call 9-1-1 if you feel CHEST DISCOMFORT that does not go away
                 with rest or medicine."

So the retrieved set contains EXACTLY ONE escalation trigger and it is
conditioned on a symptom the case explicitly denies. The tuned model reads that
correctly and says so, in its own words, run 1:

  "The retrieved context says to call 9-1-1 if chest discomfort does not go
   away with rest, AND DOES NOT SAY TO CALL FOR ANYTHING ELSE."
  "The retrieved context lists chest discomfort that does not go away with rest
   as a reason to call 9-1-1, and the patient has breathlessness at rest, NOT
   CHEST DISCOMFORT."

That is the model reasoning itself out of red because the only trigger it was
given does not fire. It is not a comprehension failure. It is faithful reading
of an inadequate context.

================================================================================
3. WHAT IS MISSING: CP-ACS-006, and it ranks 5th
================================================================================
CP-ACS-006 is the chunk written for this exact presentation:

  "These symptoms can happen together with chest pain OR WITHOUT ANY CHEST PAIN."
  "It is important to call 9-1-1 if you have these symptoms."

An unconditional trigger keyed to shoulder/back pain, breathlessness, unusual
tiredness and upset stomach. With it in the prompt the tuned model says red 3/3
and quotes it: "lists those symptoms together with chest pain as the pattern of
a heart attack, and says to call 9-1-1".

Hybrid retrieval, HE01 query, top 12 of 35, alpha 0.5:

   1. CP-ACS-003   0.9656   <-- retrieved
   2. CP-PERI-002  0.8439   <-- retrieved, never cited, matches nothing in the case
   3. CP-ANG-003   0.7738   <-- retrieved, reads it down
   4. CP-PERI-001  0.6824
   5. CP-ACS-006   0.6264   *** the keyed chunk, cut by top-3
   6. CP-ANG-002   0.5978

**CP-ACS-006 loses its slot to CP-PERI-002**, a bare three-line list of sharp
pleuritic pain, fast heartbeat and fever. The case has none of those four
things. A short bare symptom list outranks a relevant prose chunk whose symptom
words are diluted by two paragraphs about hospital screening and women's
outcomes.

================================================================================
4. WHY THE BASE GOT IT RIGHT, AND WHY THAT IS WORSE
================================================================================
The base saw the IDENTICAL three chunks and said red 3/3. It did it by
asserting chest pain the case denies:

  run 1  "CHEST DISCOMFORT that does not go away with rest or medicine is a red
          flag for possible heart attack"
  run 2  "Symptoms include CHEST DISCOMFORT, shortness of breath, SWEATING,
          nausea, and sudden fatigue"
  run 3  "CHEST DISCOMFORT not relieved by rest"

The case says "There is no real chest pain" and mentions no sweating. So the
base manufactured the finding that CP-ANG-003's trigger requires, and got the
right verdict for a fabricated reason. That is a constraint 11 contradiction
sitting in the rationale, where no guard screens it: screen_red_flags covers
red_flags, not the Why. Extending grounding to the rationale is already on the
roadmap (constraint 13) and this is a second argument for it.

**So the tune did not introduce this error. It removed the blanket red that was
hiding a retrieval gap that was always there.** Under retrieval chunks the base
was right by accident on this case and the tuned model is wrong on the evidence
it was handed.

================================================================================
5. TWO SECONDARY OBSERVATIONS, not the diagnosis
================================================================================
a. **The tuned model's own steps say RED under a YELLOW verdict.** Runs 1 and 3
   return next_steps = ["Call emergency services now", "Call emergency services
   now", "Call emergency services now"] with urgency "yellow". Only the label is
   yellow; the content is red. Nothing in the app reconciles those, because
   constraint 16 fires on a profile RAISE, not on a model that contradicts
   itself.

b. **Degenerate repetition in the rationale.** Runs 1 and 3 loop the same two
   sentences five and six times. Run 1's Why is 1,700 characters of the same
   three clauses. Only on this case, only under retrieval chunks. Worth watching
   as a possible overfit artefact of a 120-pair run, on 1 of 22 cases.

================================================================================
WHAT THIS IS NOT
================================================================================
Not a tune regression in category assignment: matched chunks give red 3/3.
Not pericarditis: CP-PERI-002 is never cited.
Not the prompt: base and tuned ran the same prompt at sha 26e4f4ff.
Not sampling: 3 of 3 in both directions, temperature 0.2.

It is retrieval returning a chunk set in which no evidence for a red exists.
Nothing changed. Viraj's call.
