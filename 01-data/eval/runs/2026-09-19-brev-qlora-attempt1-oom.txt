nothing dropped, 30 optimizer steps planned. The model loaded in 4-bit at 3.22
GB and LoRA matched 50 of 50. The failure is activation memory, not the adapter.

  Tried to allocate 18.00 GiB. 47.40 GiB capacity, 4.92 GiB free.

Two things in that, and they are separable:
  1. ACTIVATIONS. 24 GiB of activations for a 4B model at batch 1 and about
     1,200 tokens is far more than a dense transformer would use. transformers
     5.17's nemotron_h runs reference PyTorch fallbacks for every Mamba2 kernel,
     which materialise intermediates the fused kernels never hold. 21 of the 42
     blocks are Mamba2.
  2. FRAGMENTATION. 17.97 GiB was reserved and unused, which is more than the
     18 GiB the backward asked for. PyTorch's own message names the fix.

Not retried, per Viraj's instruction: report and delete rather than debug on the
meter.

What attempt 2 should change, cheapest first:
  a. PYTORCH_CUDA_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:True. Addresses cause 2 alone.
  b. gradient_checkpointing_kwargs={"use_reentrant": False}. Checkpointing was
     requested both ways (prepare_model_for_kbit_training and SFTConfig), but
     with PEFT and the reentrant default it silently does nothing unless the
     inputs require grad. This is the likeliest single cause of 24 GiB.
  c. If both still OOM: an 80 GB card. A100 80GB is roughly $1.50 to $2.00/hr
     against $0.68, so about three times the rate for a 20 minute run.
  d. NOT shortening the prompts. Their length is the retrieved context, and
     training on shorter context than the app sends is train/serve skew.

Also found, unrelated to the OOM: this TRL's SFTConfig has no warmup_ratio, so
the run had no warmup. Use warmup_steps on the next attempt.
