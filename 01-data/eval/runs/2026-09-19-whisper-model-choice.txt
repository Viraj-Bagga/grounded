WHAT WAS MEASURED
  13 clips, 84.76 s of audio, 6 voices (Samantha en_US, Daniel en_GB,
  Karen en_AU, Rishi en_IN, Aman en_IN, Tessa en_ZA, Moira en_IE), generated
  with macOS `say` at 16 kHz mono. Clips are the three demo presets, four
  safety phrasings chosen for negation / laterality / number retention, five
  real-world phrasings taken from the scope and guard work in claude.md, and
  one 25 s rambling account with a drug name and an age in it.

  THE LIMIT OF THIS SET, SAID PLAINLY. It is text to speech: no room noise, no
  disfluency, no hesitation, no clipping, no phone mic. It is clean in a way a
  desk at a hackathon is not. It is therefore valid for RANKING models against
  each other on the same input, and it OVERSTATES absolute accuracy for every
  model on the list. It cannot see the part of the base-to-small gap that only
  appears on real noisy speech. Verifying that needs Viraj and a microphone.

  flips a guard: see constraints 11 and 13) and it did not appear once.

3. CHOSEN: ggml-base.en-q5_1.bin, CPU, one process per transcription.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  57.0 MB on disk. 265 MB peak RSS. Lowest WER on this set.
  Against small.en-q5_1 it is 3.2x smaller, 2.0x less RAM, 2.2x faster, and it
  scored no worse. Against tiny it keeps "angina", "stent" and "nitroglycerin",
  which are words this corpus is made of.
  q5_1 against f16 base: 2.10% vs 2.45%, one word in 286. That is noise, not a
  quality gain. The honest statement is NO MEASURABLE LOSS FROM QUANTISATION on
  this set, at 40% of the size and 73% of the RAM.

  WHY CPU AND NOT METAL. Metal is 2.3x faster here, but CPU is already under a
  second (table 4) and CPU is the condition every other number in this project
  is quoted in. Consistency is worth more than 0.4 s nobody can see. The
  binary takes -ng, so this is one flag if it ever needs to change.

  x_realtime is the wrong way round to read this. The wall time has a FLOOR of
  about 0.6 s and grows slowly, because whisper always encodes a fixed 30 s
  window whatever the clip length. Read it as: under 1 s for anything a person
  says in one breath, 1.5 s for a 25 s ramble. Peak RSS is flat at ~260 MB and
  does not grow with clip length.

  LOAD AND UNLOAD, SETTLED BY MEASUREMENT. whisper-cli's own timings on the
  CPU path: model load 45 to 52 ms, encode 358 to 401 ms, total compute 568 to
  1443 ms. Loading the model costs 46 ms. There is no case for holding 265 MB
  resident next to a 3 GB LLM to save 46 ms. One process per transcription,
  which also means the memory is gone the moment it returns.

6. BROWSER-SIDE AUDIO CONVERSION, so the server needs no ffmpeg.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  MediaRecorder in Chrome gives webm/opus at 48 kHz. whisper.cpp wants 16 kHz
  mono PCM. Tested decodeAudioData -> OfflineAudioContext(1, n, 16000) -> WAV
  header written in JS, in real headless Chrome, on webm/opus files made with
  ffmpeg to match what MediaRecorder produces.

    p1  6.18 s: 16000 Hz mono, 193 KB, converted in 168 ms
    l1 25.00 s: 16000 Hz mono, 781 KB, converted in 107 ms

  Transcripts through the browser path are BYTE-IDENTICAL to transcripts of the
  original WAV, on both clips, including "Ramapril" and "62". So the conversion
  is lossless for this purpose and the demo server gains no new dependency.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Real speech. Every clip is synthetic. Accuracy on a real voice at a noisy
    desk is unmeasured and will be worse than 2.10% for every model here.
  - Whether base.en is still the right pick on real audio. The usual result is
    that small.en pulls ahead once the audio is dirty. This set cannot see it.
    Swapping is one env var, so it stays a 30 second experiment.
  - Anything about a phone. This is the laptop, same as the rest of the demo.
  - CoreML and the Apple Neural Engine, which whisper.cpp supports through a
    separate build and would matter on a real handset. Not built, not measured.

WHAT IS AND IS NOT HANDLED
  The parenthesised ones never reach the screen: voice.clean() returns empty
  when the whole transcript is bracketed non-speech annotation, and the page
  then says "Nothing was heard". A BARE HALLUCINATED SENTENCE HAS NOTHING
  ABOUT IT TO CATCH, which is the dangerous shape and the one the beep
  produced five times out of five.

  voice.SILENCE_PEAK = 0.01 now refuses audio below that, with "nothing
  reached the microphone". It is 78x below the quietest clip here, so it
  cannot refuse a quiet voice, and it deterministically catches the case that
  actually happens: a muted or dead microphone.

  IT DOES NOT CATCH A HALLUCINATION FROM REAL ROOM NOISE. Nothing here claims
  to. What catches that is that the transcript goes in the composer for the
  person to read and fix before sending, which is the design and not a
  consolation. Verified after the floor was added:

    silence     peak 0.0000  REFUSED before the model saw it
    noise-tiny  peak 0.0044  REFUSED before the model saw it
    noise-low   peak 0.0457  text=''   (annotation dropped)
    noise-mid   peak 0.2063  text=''   (annotation dropped)
    p3          peak 0.7791  760 ms    "a bit of indigestion after lunch..."
    p1          peak 0.8318  969 ms    "heavy pressure in the middle of my chest..."
    l1          peak 0.9275  1735 ms   "Well, it started yesterday evening..."

  NOT TUNED: whisper-cli's -nth (no-speech threshold) is left at its default
  0.60. Raising it would make the model likelier to emit nothing, but tuning
  it without a real-speech set is guesswork and the error it would buy is
  suppressing quiet real speech, which is the wrong direction.

9. CHROME 153 CANNOT PLAY A FILE INTO ITS FAKE MICROPHONE (headless)
================================================================================
  --use-file-for-fake-audio-capture was tried in every combination:
    with and without --use-fake-device-for-media-capture
    16 kHz and 48 kHz source files
    with and without the %noloop suffix
    relative and absolute paths
  All of them fed the page Chrome's built-in beep. --headless=old, which used
  to honour the flag, is removed in Chrome 153.0.8010.48.

  So `ui_check.mjs voice` replaces the audio SOURCE at page level instead:
  navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia is overridden to return a stream from a
  decoded WAV through a MediaStreamAudioDestinationNode. Everything downstream
  is real: the button, MediaRecorder, the webm encode, decodeAudioData, the
  16 kHz resample, the WAV writer, the POST, whisper.cpp and the composer.
  NOT covered: getUserMedia itself and the browser permission prompt. That is
  the one thing at the desk that needs a human to have clicked Allow once, so
  it is on the pre-demo checklist rather than in a test.

THE ROOM IS REAL, AND IT SHOWS IN THE LEVELS
  synthetic set    peak 0.779 to 0.928    rms 0.109 to 0.164
  real voice       peak 0.395 to 0.543    rms 0.0485 to 0.0651
  About half the peak and a third of the rms. This is the input the synthetic
  set could not produce, which is the whole reason for the re-run. Well clear
  of the 0.01 silence floor.

AS SCORED
  model              WER%   errs   words
  base.en            4.63      5     108
  small.en-q5_1      5.56      6     108
  small.en           5.56      6     108
  base.en-q5_1       6.48      7     108     <- the model that ships

  READ THE ERRORS BEFORE READING THE RANKING. Two of base.en-q5_1's seven are
  "I'm" for "I am" in extra2. That is a CONTRACTION, not a mishearing: it
  changes no meaning and no guard. Both small variants make it too. base.en
  alone wrote "I am", and that single difference is the whole reason it sits
  at the top of this table.

WITH THAT CONTRACTION DISCOUNTED, WHICH IS THE HONEST COMPARISON
  base.en           5 errors   4.63%
  base.en-q5_1      5 errors   4.63%
  small.en-q5_1     4 errors   3.70%
  small.en          4 errors   3.70%

  So on real audio SMALL IS AHEAD BY ONE WORD IN 108, and q5_1 costs base
  nothing at all. The ranking in the table above is an artefact of a
  contraction; this is what the models actually did.

WHERE THAT ONE WORD IS, AND WHY IT DOES NOT BUY small.en A PLACE
  extra1, said: "...I am also on ramipril for my blood pressure."
    base.en-q5_1    "I am also on Raimi Pro for my blood pressure"
    base.en         "I am also on Ramy Pro for my blood pressure"
    small.en-q5_1   "I am also on remupril for my blood pressure"
    small.en        "I am also on remupril for my blood pressure"

  NOBODY GETS ramipril RIGHT. Small renders it as one wrong word; base splits
  it into two, which is the extra error. Small is closer, not correct.

  THE SYNTHETIC SET OVERSTATED SMALL. There, small got BOTH nitroglycerin and
  ramipril exactly right and base got neither, which looked like a clean
  two-word advantage on clinical vocabulary and was the one argument for
  spending 181 MB. On a real voice that advantage collapses to a fraction of
  one word: both families get "nitroglycerin" right, and neither gets
  "ramipril". The case for small was built on `say` output and did not
  survive contact with a microphone.

  Also on preset2 the two families made DIFFERENT single errors, not a
  different number of them: base "Short" for "Sharp", small "wears" for
  "worse". The safety-miss column in the run file reads 3 for small against 2
  for base only because that list contains "worse" and not "sharp". That is
  the word list, not a finding, and it should not be read as small being
  worse on safety words.

THE MISS THAT WOULD HAVE MATTERED DID NOT HAPPEN, IN ANY MODEL
  extra2, said: "I am not pregnant. No fever, no cough. It is not the left
  side, it is the right side, and it started three days ago."

  All four models kept every one: not x2, no x2, left x1, right x1, three x1.
  A dropped "not" makes `excluded_subject` miss a pregnancy denial or makes a
  patient who says "No fever" acquire one (constraints 11 and 13). A swapped
  left and right is a fabricated finding nothing downstream can catch. None of
  it happened, on the quietest recording in the set, in any of the four.

DECISION: base.en-q5_1. Viraj's call, and now on evidence rather than on the
  assumption that base would hold.
    57 MB against 181 MB          3.2x
    265 MB RSS against 543 MB     2.0x
    0.4 s against 1.1 s per clip  2.5x on this set
  bought for one word in 108, on a drug name NEITHER model transcribes
  correctly, in an app that does not prescribe and that puts every word in an
  editable box before it is sent. If the corpus ever grows a medication
  section, this is the first thing to re-open.

ONE TIMING IS NOT A MODEL PROPERTY
  base.en-q5_1 on preset1 reads 14277 ms. The next line, base.en on the same
  clip, is 414 ms, and every other base.en-q5_1 clip is 321 to 437 ms. That is
  a cold first invocation of the run, with the model file not yet in the page
  cache. Another instance was also running llama-server generations at the
  time, so contention may be in it as well. Do not quote 14.2 s as a
  transcription time: the warm figures in this file and in
  06-demo/results/2026-09-19-voice-live.txt are the real ones.
