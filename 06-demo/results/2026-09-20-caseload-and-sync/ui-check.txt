1. BASE DID NOT SHOW ARRIVALS. Not a polling bug: base saw an arrival in under
   2 s every time. It was BASE-DESIGN.md 4.7 implemented too literally, holding
   EVERY arrival behind a "N new since you opened this" bar, so the closing
   beat showed a thin grey line and a tally still reading 44 while base held 45.
   Arrivals now land immediately, row and counts together, and are held ONLY
   while a control here has focus or the pointer is over a row. Measured: idle
   45->46 in 2 s; focused, held for the full 12 s.
   The hold also used to cancel itself, because rendering the bar destroyed the
   focused button it was protecting. The bar is now inserted in place.

2. LAN SYNC WORKS. base_server.py --lan binds *:8781, answers on
   10.6.13.168:8781, and the field device POSTed two assessments across it.
   FOUND WHILE CHECKING: saving the base address from the page WIPED the worker
   name, because the form sent no worker and set_base overwrote it with "".
   It now only writes the name when one is supplied, and the page has a field
   for it, so it is settable rather than dead config.

3. TAPPING ASSESS. Could not reproduce literal disappearance: the entry stayed
   waiting with the assessment linked. What is real is that tapping Assess
   creates NO assessment (the conversation is made on send), so for the whole
   window between the tap and the typing there was nothing to link and the row
   looked untouched, on a list the worker had just been navigated away from.
   The entry now records that work started, independent of any assessment:
     waiting -> in progress (the tap) -> seen (the assessment finishes)
     in progress -> waiting (abandoned, after 30 minutes)
   Derived, never stored, reconciled before every read, so it cannot go stale.

4. MEDICATION SCHEDULE. The form wrote it and nothing displayed it. Now shown
   on the person's record with the patient ID and when they were last seen,
   beside the facts the model reads and clearly not part of them. The
   medications list is untouched and selftest_profile_text is 25/25.

5. REFUSALS COULD NEVER SYNC. Diagnosed by the other instance and confirmed
   here. pending() tested `reached_model`, which on real data is an exact
   synonym for `kind == "result"`: over 78 saved assessments every turn is
   either ('result', True) or ('refused', False), no other combination. So the
   rule quietly meant "only send answers" and 16 refusals had never been
   sendable. Base's register counts refused as needing review, so those cases
   were counted on a screen they could never reach. All 16 have now crossed.
   The second half of that diagnosis, that base's case page would blank or
   throw on a refusal, does NOT reproduce: it renders the hold mark, OUT OF
   SCOPE and the refusal message. turnHTML already maps a refused turn.

6. BASE COUNTED TWO THINGS WITH ONE WORD. counts.red counted SIDES and the
   page's tally counted ROWS, so a compare assessment with two red sides made
   the API say 39 and the page say 38. Rows is the unit a supervisor thinks in,
   because a row is what they open. Now both say 38. Files, distinct ids, rows
   and counts.assessments all agree at 75: resends overwrite by device and id.
