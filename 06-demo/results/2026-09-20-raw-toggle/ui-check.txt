2026-09-20  "Show what the model is writing" did nothing when a person pressed it

REPORTED by Viraj. Reproduced, and the reason no check had caught it is the
reason it was invisible: every check pressed it with a synthetic .click(),
which fires instantly.

THE BUG. paint() did el.innerHTML = liveHTML(k) on a 250 ms tick, replacing
the whole live subtree. Measured: 24 DOM mutations a second. A real mouse
press holds for about 140 ms, so the button was routinely destroyed between
mousedown and mouseup, and the browser then never dispatches click at all.
The handler was fine and never ran.

THE FIX. paint() now replaces only .steps4 and writes the raw text into the
existing <pre>, leaving the button node alone. A full replace happens only
when the block appears, disappears or is toggled, none of which happens
under the pointer. Verified: the node survives 1.5 s of repaints, a 150 ms
press opens it, the text keeps streaming while open (210 -> 456 chars), the
steps keep updating, and a second press closes it.

THE CHECK. ui_check gained press(), which holds the button for 140 ms across
a repaint. Proven to catch the bug: with the old paint() restored, 2 of the 3
new assertions fail.
