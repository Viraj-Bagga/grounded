  112 pass, 0 fail across 12 modes.

WHAT WAS REVERTED, AND WHY. The glass direction landed on exactly the warm
cream the frontend-design skill warns is this year's commonest generated-page
tell, and floating every surface on a coloured ground read as unfinished
rather than as light. Viraj's call, and he is right: the four files went back
to main rather than being unpicked rule by rule, which is cleaner and leaves
nothing of the direction behind. `grep backdrop-filter` over both stylesheets
returns nothing.

KEPT FROM THAT WORK, on his instruction, because neither belonged to the glass:
  ink-3 #5c6572 -> #5c6472. On THIS palette it measures 5.96 on paper, 5.08 in
    a well and 5.41 on chrome. The original already cleared AA here; the
    darkening was for the greige ground and is kept as harmless headroom. The
    comment in app.css carried the greige numbers for one commit and now
    carries the measured ones for this palette.
  The top bar fix. It rendered as a 1px strip with two icons overflowing it on
    every desktop screen, because the base .top rule sits after the media query
    that hides it and display:none was losing. Pre-existing, in every
    screenshot taken today, and nothing to do with the revamp.

NEW, from his direction:
  The empty screen is bottom-aligned. The presets and the scope lines sat
    centred in a void; they now sit directly above the composer with an
    ordinary gap. Empty space collects at the top, where it costs nothing.
  The presets are rows divided by hairlines, not three bordered cards. They
    read as ways to start rather than as a control panel.

UNTOUCHED, as instructed: the urgency bars, in every state.
