========================================================================
TWO CORRECTIONS AND ONE FINDING, ADDED AFTER THE RUN
========================================================================

1. STEP 5's "INTEGRITY: MISMATCH" IS WRONG. It is a bug in this harness, not
   in the pack install. The node namespaces its packs: packs.py's node_id()
   maps a region id to "regional-<id>", and the harness looked the pack up by
   the bare region id, found nothing, and compared a real hash against
   undefined. Checked by hand against the live node with the right id:

     installed, recomputed from this device's files: 2ae230ac1803cd88c7ca1491fa0750a3…
     published by the node:                          2ae230ac1803cd88c7ca1491fa0750a3…
     INTEGRITY: MATCH

   rehearsal.mjs is fixed and the fixed lookup was verified against the live
   node for both packs. The number in the step above is left as it was
   printed rather than edited, because a results file that quietly agrees with
   itself afterwards is worth nothing.

2. "per-file sha256 lines on screen: 1" undercounts. The page shows each
   file's hash as it lands during the install and then collapses to the
   pack-level summary once it is done, so counting after the fact sees one.
   The live per-file verification is in 06-demo/results/2026-09-19-packs/.

3. FINDING, not a break: A FOLLOW-UP HIDES THE FIRST TURN'S RULE AND REMOVALS
   FROM THE BASE REGISTER. Aunt Sue's row shows "—" in Attention. Her first
   turn was raised to red by R4 with 5 guard removals; her follow-up was a
   plain red with none. base_server.row() summarises the LAST turn per side,
   so the register shows R4:supports and 0 removed, and the detail page shows
   the last turn too.

     turn 1  result  red  rules=['R4:raised']   dropped={next_steps: 4, rationale: 1}
     turn 2  result  red  rules=['R4:supports'] dropped={}
     register row: rules=['R4:supports'] removed=0  ->  Attention renders "—"

   The data is all at base; the summary drops it. It matters twice over: a
   supervisor scanning the register cannot see that an assessment was
   escalated or modified, and the OUTSTANDING rule counts `removed`, so an
   assessment whose only flag was a guard removal on an earlier turn stops
   being outstanding once a follow-up lands. Not fixed. Viraj's call whether
   the row should summarise the WHOLE assessment rather than its last turn.
