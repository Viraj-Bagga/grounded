THE ONE FAILURE WAS MINE AND THE CHECK EXISTS FOR EXACTLY IT.

  FAIL  compare: record, answers and composer share one left edge  ([384,384,372])

Giving the sheet a 1.75rem inset at 60rem left the composer's inner box on the
old --pad maths, so its content sat 12px left of the record and the answers.
The first fix made it worse (28px), because the check measures the BOX edge of
.composer .in, not its content: the bar has to be as wide as the sheet's text
COLUMN, not as wide as the sheet.

Fixed by making the inset a token. --sheet-pad: 1.75rem, and the bar is sized
calc(--col - 2 * --sheet-pad) from it, so the two cannot drift apart again.
That drift is precisely what broke it.

ALSO FIXED, found by arithmetic rather than by a check: ink-3 on the new greige
ground measured 4.27:1, under AA for normal text, and .scope .how on the empty
screen is ink-3 at 12px sitting directly on the ground. ink-3 went #616977 to
#5c6472: 4.61 on the ground, 5.76 on paper, 4.96 in a well. Every other pair in
the palette was already clear, including all four urgency bars, which are
unchanged.
