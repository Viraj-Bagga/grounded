the distribution node existed only in a terminal transcript. The pack now
arrives over HTTP and the page shows it happening and keeps the evidence.

THE VERIFICATION IS NOT REIMPLEMENTED. packs.install calls client.cmd_pull with
its stdout captured line by line. That is the reference client running exactly
as it ships: index, manifest, per-file download, hash FROM DISK, pack_sha256
recomputed from the files, manifest.json written LAST. Nothing in 06-demo
decides whether a pack is good.

            Start it with: python 07-distribute/server.py

  AN INSTALLED PACK DOES NOT STOP WORKING WHEN THE NODE GOES AWAY. That is the
  point of a pack, and the page has to behave that way or the claim is empty.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. INTEGRITY FAILURE: ALREADY COVERED, NOT REBUILT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Two different failures, and only one of them reaches the client:

  A FILE THAT GOES BAD ON THE NODE'S OWN DISK never reaches a client at all.
  Measured here: a byte appended to citations.csv in a copy of the packs
  directory, manifest untouched, and the node WITHDREW regional-africa-ssa from
  /packs by itself. The client then reports it is not on the node. That is the
  node's "verified before advertised" rule doing its job.

  A BIT FLIPPED IN TRANSIT is the client's job and 07-distribute/selftest.py
  already covers it: T06 "a bit flipped in transit is refused", with a
  poisoning proxy between client and node. Not rebuilt here. The page renders
  that outcome as a hatched panel reading "The bytes did not match the
  manifest. Nothing was installed.", in the same hold grey as every other
  refusal, never in a triage colour.
