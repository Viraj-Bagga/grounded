Atkinson Hyperlegible Next
The system face this page would fall back to
Built by the Braille Institute for readers with low vision. Every character that is easy to confuse gets its own shape: a tailed l, a serifed I, a flagged 1, a slashed zero. A person reading on a small phone in bad light, worried, is the reader it was drawn for.
The citation guard matches keys character by character, and a person may copy a key from the screen. In the mono twin, CP-ACS-001 cannot be misread as CP-ACS-00l.
Considered and left: IBM Plex Sans, which is Carbon’s own, set aside on 27 September when you took Carbon’s structure and not its type; and Public Sans, the US government’s face, a natural match for government sources, but its I and l are nearly the same stroke.

## What changes from today
What changes from today
